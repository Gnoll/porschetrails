The [Crown Range Road](/trails/crown-range-road/) is the direct route between Queenstown and Wānaka, and at the Crown Saddle it reaches 1,076 m. A plaque at the top calls it the highest sealed road in New Zealand. The route is old, but the last gravel section was only sealed in 2001.

Late October is when it comes into season. Winter brings snow and ice to the saddle, and the usual advice is to carry chains from May to September. Spring days are longer and the high tussock is clear, but the road is still high enough for a cold snap to bring frost or snow, so check the forecast and the Queenstown Lakes District Council's road updates before you set off.

## The drive

Start at Arrow Junction, just south of Arrowtown. The first section is the zigzag up to the Crown Terrace: steep, tight hairpins, mostly first and second gear, with the valley floor dropping away below. Above the terrace the road opens out across bare hillside towards the summit, where the lookout gives a view back over the Wakatipu basin.

The Cardrona side is a different road. It runs down a long, gentle valley past the small settlement of Cardrona and out towards Wānaka, with flowing bends and long sight lines. It is a pleasant, relaxed descent rather than a demanding one.

## In your Porsche

The hairpins on the Queenstown side are tight enough that a 911 needs all of its lane, and a mid-engined [Boxster or Cayman](/models/718-981/) feels especially at home there. Take them at a tidy pace: you are sharing them with rental campervans, many of them driven by people new to mountain roads and to driving on the left.

Shaded corners near the summit hold frost later into the morning than the valley does. On a cold spring day, summer tyres have little grip until the road warms up, so a late-morning start is no hardship.

## Making a day of it

The Crown Range itself is short, so treat it as the start of something longer. From Wānaka, the Haast Pass heads west to the coast and the Lindis Pass runs north towards Mount Cook country. Both are faster and emptier than the Crown Range, and both make good full days from a Wānaka base.
