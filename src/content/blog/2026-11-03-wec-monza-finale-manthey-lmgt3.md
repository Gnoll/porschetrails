The FIA World Endurance Championship ends its 2026 season this weekend at Monza, and a Porsche is still in the fight for a title. The #92 The Bend Manthey 911 GT3 R goes into the 6 Hours of Monza on Sunday 8 November second in the LMGT3 standings, 18 points behind the leaders, according to [Porsche Motorsport's November preview](https://racing.porsche.com/articles/november-monthly-preview-2026).

## Why the season ends in Italy

Monza was not on the original calendar. In July the WEC cancelled its Qatar and Bahrain rounds because of the situation in the Middle East, and replaced them with a new race at Barcelona in October and a second visit to Monza as the finale ([Racer](https://racer.com/2026/07/28/wec-confirms-barcelona-and-monza-races-to-replace-bahrain-and-qatar)). The change also shortens the last race: Bahrain was an eight-hour event, while Monza runs for six. The track is 5.793 km long, with long straights and heavy braking into its chicanes.

## The #92 crew

Richard Lietz, Riccardo Pera and Yasser Shahin share the car. Their LMGT3 win at the 6 Hours of Barcelona on 18 October was the team's first victory of the season ([Pit Debrief](https://www.pitdebrief.com/post/tag/riccardo-pera/)), and it closed the gap enough to keep the championship open going into the final round. Manthey has won the last two LMGT3 titles and is aiming for a third in a row.

The car is the racing version of the [992-generation 911](/models/911-992/), built for customer teams. Manthey itself is based beside the Nürburgring, and its name will be familiar to anyone who has driven the [Nordschleife](/trails/nurburgring-nordschleife/) or looked at upgrade kits for a road-going GT3.

Porsche's own factory Hypercar effort concentrated on IMSA in North America this year ([Porsche Newsroom](https://newsroom.porsche.com/en/ppdb/2025/11/porsche-enters-the-2026-motorsport-year-with-a-revised-focus.html)), so the Manthey car carries the brand's hopes in the WEC.

## The rest of November

Monza is not the only Porsche racing this month. Four 911 GT3 R cars are entered in the FIA GT World Cup at the 73rd Macau Grand Prix, on the 6.120 km Guia street circuit. Factory drivers Laurin Heinrich and Thomas Preining are joined by Dorian Boccolacci and Alessio Picariello.

From 11 to 15 November, twelve young drivers nominated from the Carrera Cup series around the world take part in the Porsche Junior Shoot-Out. It starts with simulator and off-track assessments and moves to on-track trials in a 911 Cup car at Portimão. One driver will be chosen as the 2027 Porsche Junior, with the name announced at the Night of Champions in Weissach on 5 December.
