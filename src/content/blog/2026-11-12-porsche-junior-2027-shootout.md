Porsche Motorsport has named the twelve drivers competing to become the 2027 Porsche Junior. The candidates are aged 18 to 22, come from ten countries, and were each nominated by one of the national [Carrera Cup](/events/#porsche-carrera-cup) championships. The selection was announced on 11 November in the [Porsche Newsroom](https://newsroom.porsche.com/en/2026/motorsports/porsche-junior-2027-young-racing-driver-preview-43703.html).

## How the shootout works

The selection runs over four days in two places. The first part takes place at the Porsche Esports Performance Centre in Cologne, where the candidates go through interviews, tests, simulator sessions and assessments. The second part moves to the Autódromo Internacional do Algarve at Portimão, the 4.563 km circuit in southern Portugal, for the driving tests.

The car is the 992.2-generation 911 Cup, rated at 382 kW (520 PS). It is the same car already used in 2026 in the Porsche Mobil 1 Supercup and in selected Carrera Cups, so every candidate arrives with some experience of a [992-based](/models/911-992/) racing 911.

## The candidates

- Antonella Bassani (Brazil), Carrera Cup Brasil
- Macéo Capietto (France), Carrera Cup France
- Pietro Delli Guanti (Italy), Carrera Cup Italia
- Matheus Ferreira (Brazil), Carrera Cup Deutschland
- Marco Giltrap (New Zealand), Carrera Cup Asia
- Milan Marczak (Poland), Carrera Cup Benelux
- Tyler Maxson (USA), Carrera Cup North America
- Clay Osborne (New Zealand), Carrera Cup Australia
- William Siverholm (Sweden), Carrera Cup Scandinavia
- Caleb Sumich (Australia), Carrera Cup Middle East
- Callum Voisin (Great Britain), Carrera Cup Great Britain
- Taichi Watarai (Japan), Carrera Cup Japan

Maxson is the driver who took the Carrera Cup North America title at Circuit of the Americas last month. Bassani is the only woman in the group. Siverholm also took part in the 2025 selection, and Capietto already has two LMP2 starts at the 24 Hours of Le Mans.

## What the winner gets

The new Junior receives financial support for the 2027 season in the [Porsche Mobil 1 Supercup](/events/#porsche-supercup), which will run at eight European Grand Prix events. Porsche Motorsport adds a development package covering driving dynamics, mental strength, nutrition, fitness and media work.

A parallel shootout for five Porsche Esports Supercup finalists uses the same facilities, ending in a 911 Challenge car at Portimão. Its winner gets a season in a national Porsche one-make series.

## Where past Juniors went

The programme began in 1997 and has supported 34 drivers. Several have gone on to the very top of sports car racing. Timo Bernhard, Marc Lieb and Earl Bamber all won Le Mans and the FIA World Endurance Championship. Thomas Preining, Junior in 2017 and 2018, won his second DTM title this year. Laurin Heinrich, Junior in 2022, won at Daytona and Sebring in 2026 in the 963.
