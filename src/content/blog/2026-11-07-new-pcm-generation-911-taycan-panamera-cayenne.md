On 3 November Porsche announced a new generation of Porsche Communication Management (PCM), the infotainment system in the centre of the dashboard. It is coming to the [911](/models/911-992/), the [Taycan](/models/taycan/), the Panamera and the petrol and hybrid Cayenne.

## Faster hardware

The system runs on a new chip architecture that Porsche says has up to five times the computing power of the previous generation. The claimed benefits are quicker start-up, shorter loading times and smoother switching between functions. Apps can keep running in the background and reopen faster. Porsche also says the new platform is built to take future functions, which matters because the system receives over-the-air updates: new software versions and selected features can be sent to the car after delivery.

## The Cayenne Electric's screens, spread across the range

The layout and design language, which Porsche calls Porsche Digital Interaction, first appeared in the Cayenne Electric. It now moves to the other four model lines. Its most visible feature is a 3D model of your own car, shown in its actual paint colour, which you can rotate to reach some settings and functions. There are five selectable themes and configurable widgets.

Navigation Plus uses online map data with live traffic and a new 3D map view. In the electric models the Porsche Charging Planner plans charging stops and allows for preconditioning the battery on the way.

## Voice Pilot and apps

The Voice Pilot assistant is now based on a large language model. Porsche says it understands natural speech, multi-step requests and follow-up questions, and can control navigation, media, selected apps and selected vehicle functions.

An App Center lets owners install and update Porsche and third-party apps, including streaming, navigation, parking and charging apps. For the first time, Apple Music is available in the car through the App Center. In selected markets it can play Spatial Audio with Dolby Atmos, provided the car has a Dolby Atmos-capable Bose or Burmester 3D High-End sound system and the owner has an Apple Music subscription. Smartphone integration is also extended, with the phone's map able to appear in the instrument cluster.

## What we do not know yet

Porsche says the system will be introduced gradually in newly built cars, with availability depending on market, model, production date and specification. It has not given model years or a start date for each line. The announcement also says nothing about whether cars already on the road can be upgraded, so owners of a current 911 or Taycan should not assume they will get it.
