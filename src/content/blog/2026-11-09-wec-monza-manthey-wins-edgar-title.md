The World Endurance Championship season ended at Monza on Sunday 8 November with a Porsche on top of the LMGT3 results, but not on top of the table. The #92 The Bend Manthey 911 GT3 R of Yasser Shahin, Riccardo Pera and Richard Lietz won the class at the 6 Hours of Monza, crossing the line 0.270 seconds ahead of the #23 Heart of Racing Aston Martin ([Sportscar365](https://sportscar365.com/lemans/wec/jota-cadillac-wins-monza-finale-no-8-toyota-takes-title)). Third place for the #33 TF Sport Corvette was enough for Jonny Edgar to take the LMGT3 drivers' title, and TF Sport won the teams' title ([FIA WEC](https://www.fiawec.com/en/race/6-hours-of-monza-2026)).

As we noted in [our preview last week](/blog/2026-11-03-wec-monza-finale-manthey-lmgt3/), the #92 crew arrived 18 points behind the leaders after winning in Barcelona. Monza made it two class wins in a row, but it was not enough.

## How the race was decided

It was a messy race in LMGT3, with several collisions, safety car periods and drive-through penalties for rival teams. The Corvette crew had their own setback early on: Ben Keating was given a penalty in the second hour for a virtual safety car infringement, which dropped the #33 out of the points for a time.

The class was settled by strategy. When the fourth virtual safety car of the race came out, the #92 Porsche and the #33 Corvette were the only two front-running cars still to make their stop, which gave both a large advantage. A Ford that might have challenged was then damaged in contact with a Toyota and had to pit for repairs. That left Lietz in front for the run to the flag, with the Aston Martin closing but finishing just short.

The second Manthey Porsche finished sixth in class, according to the classification published by [gt-place.com](https://gt-place.com/2026/11/08/fia-wec-manthey-sieg-beim-saisonfinale-jonny-edgar-lmgt3-meister/).

## A milestone for Lietz

Monza was Richard Lietz's 100th WEC race, and he marked it with a class win.

## The end of a run

Manthey won the LMGT3 title in each of its first two seasons, 2024 and 2025. Edgar's championship ends that run and gives Corvette the 2026 crown. In the Hypercar class, the #38 Cadillac of Jack Aitken, Sébastien Bourdais and Earl Bamber won the race, and the #8 Toyota crew took the drivers' title. Porsche's factory Hypercar team was not on the grid this year, having concentrated on IMSA in North America.

## Where the car comes from

The 911 GT3 R is the customer racing version of the [992-generation 911](/models/911-992/). Manthey is based beside the Nürburgring, so owners who have driven the [Nordschleife](/trails/nurburgring-nordschleife/) will know the name.
