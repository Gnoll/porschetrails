November is the start of the best season on [Chapman's Peak Drive](/trails/chapmans-peak-drive/). The winter storms that close the road for wind and rockfall become less frequent, the evenings are long, and the Atlantic below is usually blue rather than grey. With [Franschhoek Pass](/trails/franschhoek-pass/) still closed for flood repairs, it is also the most obvious mountain road for a short drive out of Cape Town. This year, though, you will meet a set of traffic lights on the way.

## The road

Chapman's Peak Drive (the M6) runs for 9 km between Hout Bay and Noordhoek, with 114 curves cut into near-vertical cliffs. It opened in 1922 and was rebuilt in the early 2000s with rockfall shelters and catch fences. It is a toll road. From Hout Bay it climbs to a lookout at its highest point, then winds down to the long beach at Noordhoek. The corners are tight and often blind, the speed limit is low, and the whole thing takes about twenty minutes. It is a road to look at, not a road to hurry.

## Roadworks until March 2027

Structural and major repair works on the route are scheduled from 26 October 2026 to 31 March 2027. A notice published on 9 November says the work is between Lookout Point and the end of the Half Tunnel, the most dramatic part of the drive, and that a stop-and-go system is in place. Motorists are told to expect minor delays and to allow extra time. Cyclists are asked to follow the signals and signs through the work zone.

None of the reports we found mention a full closure for the works, but Chapman's Peak can still close at short notice for high wind, heavy rain or rockfall. The operator's website carries a live road status banner. Check it on the day, and expect to wait at the boards near the top.

## How to plan a drive

Early morning is the best time. Sunset brings heavy traffic, and weekend mornings bring a lot of cyclists, who now share a single controlled lane with you near Lookout Point. Patience at the boards is part of the deal until the end of March.

The road works best as part of a peninsula loop. Leave the city through Camps Bay and along Victoria Road to Hout Bay, cross Chapman's Peak, continue south to Cape Point, and return along the False Bay coast through Simon's Town. That makes a relaxed half day, with time to stop.

Spring is also a good moment to get a car ready for summer use. If it has been sitting through the Cape winter, check tyre pressures and condition before you go.
