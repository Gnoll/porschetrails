A month ago we wrote about the queues on [Irohazaka](/trails/irohazaka/) in leaf season. The colour has now moved south. Around Hakone, south-west of Tokyo, it is turning this month, and the [Hakone Turnpike](/trails/hakone-turnpike/) is the best way up into it.

## The road

The Turnpike is a privately run toll road, now signed as the Anest Iwata Turnpike Hakone. It climbs about 15.8 km from Odawara, near sea level, to Daikanzan at roughly 1,000 m. Unlike the tight mountain passes elsewhere in Japan, it is wide and smooth, with long uphill sweepers through forest. Japanese manufacturers use it to show new cars to the press for that reason.

Between roughly the 2 km and 7 km markers the road is lined with cherry trees. They are the reason for the spring crowds (and for our photo). In November the same stretch is quieter, and the colour comes from the mixed woodland around it.

## When the colour arrives

Hakone's leaves turn from the top of the mountain downwards over three to four weeks. Planning guides based on the Japan Meteorological Corporation forecast give a rough order for 2026:

- **Owakudani and Sengokuhara:** late October to mid-November.
- **Lake Ashi and Gora:** mid-November.
- **Hakone-Yumoto, at the bottom of the valley:** late November to early December.

These are forecasts, and the timing moves from year to year with the weather. Check a current leaf report before you set off.

## Timing your drive

The Turnpike carries far fewer coaches than Irohazaka, but Hakone is still one of the busiest day trips from Tokyo in November, and the roads around Lake Ashi and Yumoto get very slow at weekends.

- **Go early.** The Turnpike is quiet before eight, and the Daikanzan car park is a good place to stop while it is still empty.
- **Remember it closes overnight.** The toll road has opening hours, so check them before planning a dawn run.
- **Keep to the limit.** Police patrol at weekends and the limits are low by European standards. The views are the reason to come in November.
- **Watch for fog at the top.** Cloud often sits on the ridge, and frost and ice become possible near the summit later in the season.

## At the top

On a clear morning Daikanzan looks across Lake Ashi to Mount Fuji. From there the Ashinoko Skyline runs along the ridge above the lake on a tighter, more technical road, and the Izu Skyline continues south down the peninsula.

Hakone is also one of the best places in Japan to see Porsches. Sunday mornings at Daikanzan often bring a good showing of air-cooled 911s from the [964](/models/911-964/) and earlier.
