The sixth Icons of Porsche festival takes place in Dubai on Saturday 21 and Sunday 22 November. Over the past fortnight Porsche Middle East has confirmed several of the cars that will be there.

## A new venue

The festival has moved to The Square, Nad Al Sheba, after last year's edition at Dubai Design District. Tickets cost AED 85 from IconsOfPorsche.com, and children under 12 can register for a free ticket when they come with a ticketed adult ([Porsche Newsroom Middle East](https://newsroom.porsche.com/en_PME/2026/company/porsche-icons-dubai-2026-43391.html)). Porsche says last year's edition drew more than 30,000 visitors and over 1,000 sports cars, and it expects similar numbers this year. Check the festival site for opening hours before you set off.

## Race cars from Stuttgart

The Porsche Museum is sending race cars to Dubai, picked because they inspired the Mission S concept that Porsche revealed in New York last month ([we covered it here](/blog/2026-10-16-porsche-mission-s-revealed/)). Porsche names three of them ([Porsche Newsroom Middle East](https://newsroom.porsche.com/en_PME/2026/company/porsche-museum-icons-of-porsche-mission-s-inspirations-43709.html)):

- a 1954 550 A Spyder, which Porsche describes as its first car built for motor racing;
- a 1970 917 KH Coupé;
- the 1998 911 GT1-98R, chassis GT1 001, the revised GT1 with Porsche's first carbon-fibre chassis.

Porsche says the concept also draws on the [356](/models/356/). Locally owned classics will share the space, including a 959, G-series 911s, a 928 and 356s. Our guide to [famous Porsche cars](/guides/famous-porsche-cars/) has the background on the 917 and the 550.

## The 924 police car

The most local story is a 1979 Porsche 924 that served with the Abu Dhabi Police. Porsche Centre Abu Dhabi and Al Ain says it was acquired to escort Queen Elizabeth II and the Duke of Edinburgh during the Queen's first visit to the UAE that year, and calls it the first sports car in the UAE Federal Police fleet. The centre restored it this year at its Abu Dhabi workshop, hand-painting the police markings rather than using decals. Icons of Porsche is its first public showing, after which it goes to the Al Maqtaa Museum in Abu Dhabi ([Porsche Newsroom Middle East](https://newsroom.porsche.com/en_PME/2026/company/porsche-centre-abu-dhabi-al-ain-924-police-car-43681.html)).

## A Kuwait-only 911 Turbo S

Also on show is the 911 Turbo S Sadu Edition, designed with Porsche Exclusive Manufaktur to mark 70 years of Porsche in Kuwait. Its details draw on Al Sadu, a traditional weaving craft with roots in desert life on UNESCO's intangible heritage list. Twenty will be built, all for the Kuwaiti market ([Porsche Newsroom Middle East](https://newsroom.porsche.com/en_PME/2026/company/porsche-911-turbo-s-sadu-edition-icons-of-porsche-dubai-43636.html)).

## Making a weekend of it

November is when the Gulf becomes comfortable for driving again. If you are coming from Abu Dhabi or staying on, [Jebel Hafeet](/trails/jebel-hafeet/) near Al Ain is about 90 minutes from Dubai. For other shows and meetings this winter, see our [events calendar](/events/).
