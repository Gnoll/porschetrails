While the northern hemisphere's high passes close one by one, the southern hemisphere is heading the other way. In Victoria, the [Great Alpine Road](/trails/great-alpine-road/) runs 339 km from Wangaratta to Bairnsdale, crossing Mount Hotham at 1,840 m. Late October, with the ski crowds gone and summer's cyclists not yet arrived in force, is one of the better times to drive it.

## Why now

The 2026 snow season at Hotham was a short one. The resort confirmed that Sunday 6 September was its last day of skiing and boarding for the winter, well before the early October finish the season normally runs to. Lift queues and ski traffic on the climb from Harrietville have gone with it.

During the declared snow season, every vehicle heading up to Hotham or Dinner Plain must carry correctly fitting diamond-pattern chains, and a resort entry permit is needed for the drive into the resort. Those rules belong to the winter period. Even so, Hotham sits high enough for snow to fall well outside the ski season, so check the forecast and the resort's road conditions page before you set off, and turn back if the summit is icy.

## The drive

The climb from Harrietville is the highlight: about 30 km of tight, steep bends through tall forest, coming out above the tree line onto an exposed ridge with drops on both sides. Beyond the summit the road runs through snow gums to Dinner Plain and Omeo, then follows the Tambo River south through a long, twisting valley towards Bairnsdale.

This is a road for a steady, tidy pace rather than a fast one. Much of it is narrow and the ridge section has little room for error. A 911 suits it well, and a mid-engined [Boxster or Cayman](/models/718-981/) is especially happy on the tighter forest sections.

## What to watch for

- **Debris.** Fallen bark, branches and gravel collect on the forest sections, which is hard on paint and low front splitters.
- **Wildlife.** Wombats, wallabies and deer are active at dusk. Plan to be off the mountain before dark.
- **Fuel.** Fill up in Bright or Omeo; there is little in between.

## Making a weekend of it

Bright is the natural base, with plenty of accommodation and a friendly attitude to car groups. From there the Tawonga Gap road to Mount Beauty and the climb to Mount Buffalo make good half-day runs. Some of the higher roads and tracks in the Alpine National Park stay closed until their seasonal opening, so check Parks Victoria and VicTraffic before you plan a loop over the top.
