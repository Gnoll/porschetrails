In Europe the high passes are closing one by one. In the Gulf it works the other way round. Through the summer the road up [Jebel Hafeet](/trails/jebel-hafeet/), outside Al Ain in Abu Dhabi, is too hot to enjoy. October is when the season starts.

## Why October

Al Ain's average daily maximum is above 42°C from May to September. In October it falls to about 38°C, in November to about 31°C, and by December and January it is in the mid-twenties (Al Ain airport averages, 1991 to 2020). On a long climb in 40°C air, tyres overheat and go greasy, brakes get hot on the way down, and coolant and oil temperatures climb in the slow traffic near the top.

So October is the start of the season, not the best of it. Early in the month, go at first light. From November onwards the whole morning is usable.

## The road

The road was finished in 1980 and runs about 11.7 km up the mountain, with roughly 60 corners. It has two lanes going up and one coming down. The tarmac is wide, smooth and well cambered, and the sight lines over bare rock are about as good as a public road gets. The climb is mostly fast, constant-radius sweepers, with a few tighter hairpins. It ends at a car park and a hotel just below the summit.

It is a dead end, so you drive back down the same way, on a single lane with less room for error. Most of the heat goes into the brakes on the way down. Use a lower gear and let the engine hold the car, as we explain in our piece on [brakes on long descents](/blog/2026-10-03-ceramic-or-iron-brakes-on-long-descents/).

## What it suits

Very few public roads have a surface this good, so a firmly set-up car works here. A [992](/models/911-992/) GT3 or a [Cayman GT4](/models/718-981/) feels at home, and so does a standard Carrera. The Gulf has a large Porsche community, and the car park at the top is a regular weekend meeting point once the weather turns.

## What to watch

- **Cameras.** The mountain and every road leading to it have speed cameras. Stick to the posted limits.
- **Cyclists.** The climb is a popular training ride at dawn and dusk. The UAE Tour cycle race has also used it for stage finishes, so expect closures on race days.
- **The descent.** With only one lane, a slow car ahead is something to live with, not to pass.
- **Heat.** Even in October, carry water, and do not leave the car standing in full sun at the top for long.

Al Ain is about 90 minutes from both Dubai and Abu Dhabi. If you are driving over from either city, our [road trip preparation guide](/guides/road-trip-preparation/) covers tyres and fluids. Check pressures cold, before you set off, not at the top.
