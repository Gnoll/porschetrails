Once the salt goes down, many owners put the car away until March or April. A Porsche tolerates a winter in the garage well, provided you deal with the three things that suffer from standing: the battery, the tyres and the fuel. Here is what to do, roughly in order.

## Before it goes away

- **Wash and dry it properly**, including the wheel arches and the underside. Salt and damp leaves left on paint or in drain channels do their damage slowly. Go for a short drive afterwards so the brakes are dry when you park.
- **Change the oil if it is nearly due.** Used oil carries combustion acids and moisture. Fresh oil is better company for an engine over four months.
- **Fill the tank.** A full tank leaves less room for moist air and so less condensation. This matters most on older cars with steel tanks, such as the [air-cooled 911s](/models/911-classic/).

## The battery

This is the one that catches most people out. A modern Porsche draws a small current even when locked, for the alarm, the tracker and the control units, and a healthy battery can be flat in a few weeks.

The fix is a battery maintainer, which holds the battery at full charge without overcharging it. Porsche sells its own, the Charge-o-mat Pro, and there are good independent ones. Check the handbook for how to connect it on your car.

## Tyres and brakes

Tyres left under load in one position can develop flat spots, felt as a vibration on the first drives of spring. To reduce the risk, raise the pressures for storage (a Porsche Centre will give a figure for your car) and roll the car a short distance every few weeks. Reset the pressures before you drive.

Where the car allows it, leave the parking brake off and the car in gear or in Park, with chocks if needed. Pads left clamped to damp discs for months can stick.

## Should you start it now and then?

No, unless you are going to drive it. Idling an engine for ten minutes in the garage never gets the oil hot enough to drive off moisture, and it puts condensation into the exhaust. Either take the car out for a proper run on a dry, salt-free day, or leave it alone on the maintainer.

## Electric cars

A [Taycan](/models/taycan/) is different. Porsche's service guidance says a Taycan parked for more than a week should be left connected to suitable AC charging equipment, and that the Charge-o-mat Pro cannot be used on it. The high-voltage battery looks after the 12 V battery.
