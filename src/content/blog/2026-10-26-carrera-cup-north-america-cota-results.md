The 2026 Porsche Carrera Cup North America season ended at Circuit of the Americas in Austin, Texas, on 24 and 25 October, on the support bill at the Formula 1 United States Grand Prix. Tyler Maxson of TOPP Racing came into the weekend leading the Mobil Pro class, and left it as champion. We previewed the finale [last week](/blog/2026-10-22-carrera-cup-north-america-cota-finale/).

## Saturday: the title is settled

The first race went to Janne Stiak of ACI Motorsports, his second win of the season. Aaron Jeansonne (Kellymoss), the only driver who could still catch Maxson, finished second, with Maxson third.

Third place was enough. According to the series report, Maxson left Saturday with 300 points to Jeansonne's 272, a gap that the points still available on Sunday could not close. He said afterwards: "Third place is never necessarily what we want, we want to be two spots better."

## Sunday: Maxson finishes with a win

Maxson then won the final race by 6.077 seconds and set the fastest lap. It was his ninth win of the season and the tenth for TOPP Racing. Stiak finished second after Callum Hedge (JDX Racing), who had been fighting him for the place, was given a 10-second penalty for exceeding track limits. Jared Thomas of JTR Motorsports Engineering was third.

## The other champions

Two class titles had already been decided at Road Atlanta at the start of October, and both were confirmed at the finale:

- **Pro-Am:** Patrick Mulcahy (ACI Motorsports), who also won the class on Sunday. The series says it was his 15th class win in 16 races and his first Porsche one-make championship.
- **Masters:** Marco Cirone (ACI Motorsports).
- **Entrants:** TOPP Racing, for the second year running.

Scott Blind of Ruckus Racing won the Masters class on Sunday, leading every lap, ahead of Rob Walker and Joel Johnson.
