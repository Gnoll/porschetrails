Auto Zürich opens today at Messe Zürich and runs until Sunday 8 November. Porsche Schweiz has confirmed what it is bringing ([Porsche Newsroom Switzerland](https://newsroom.porsche.com/de_CH/2026/unternehmen/porsche-schweiz-auto-zuerich-2026-43656.html)): the 911 GT3 Bergsport, the new Cayenne Electric, the current range and a separate classic exhibition.

## The GT3 Bergsport

The car most owners will want to see is the GT3 Bergsport, a market edition from Porsche Exclusive Manufaktur. It was first shown at the Arosa ClassicCar hill climb in September and is sold only in the four Alpine countries: Switzerland, France, Italy and Austria. Porsche will build 100 ([Porsche Newsroom](https://newsroom.porsche.com/en/2026/products/porsche-gt3-bergsport-market-edition-exclusive-manufaktur-43167.html)).

It is based on the current [992-generation](/models/911-992/) 911 GT3 with the Touring Package, so there is no fixed rear wing. The specification centres on the Lightweight Package and a six-speed manual gearbox. Porsche has not announced any change to the engine.

Most of the work is visual. The paint is Valleygreen Metallic, a new Paint to Sample Plus colour with gold metallic particles. The forged wheels are finished in satin White Gold and Ceramica, and the brake calipers are orange. A chequered flag motif on the front lid refers to the 908 Bergspyder hill-climb car. Inside, the carbon bucket seats are trimmed in Valleygreen leather, and cross-stitching on the doors and dashboard forms mountain-peak outlines. A round emblem on the engine cover shows four summits, one for each of the four countries, above a winding road.

Porsche has not published a price. Our guide to [Porsche colours](/guides/porsche-colours/) explains the Paint to Sample programme.

## Cayenne Electric and the current range

The Cayenne Electric returns in production form, after visitors saw a camouflaged prototype last year. Porsche Schweiz quotes an 800-volt system and charging at up to 400 kW. The current range on the stand includes a Taycan 4S, a Panamera 4S E-Hybrid, a Macan GTS and a 911 Carrera 4S Cabriolet.

## The classic hall

Porsche Classic has its own display in Hall 6. It includes two 993-generation cars (a 911 Turbo with the WLS II power kit and a Carrera S in Vesuvius Grey), a 964 Turbo 3.6 in Amethyst, a 1972 911 2.4 S in Albert Blue and a 1975 Carrera 2.7 anniversary model, one of 484 built. There is also a 928 S4 in Coral Red that was shown at the 1991 Geneva Motor Show and is still with its first owner. If you are thinking about buying one, our [993 model guide](/models/911-993/) explains what to look for.

## Visiting

The Porsche stand is in Hall 5. The show is open from 10:00 to 21:00 on Thursday and Friday and from 10:00 to 19:00 at the weekend. Messe Zürich is a short walk from Zürich-Oerlikon station, and there is parking at the Messe car park.

The roads this car is named for, such as the [Furka, Grimsel and Susten loop](/trails/furka-grimsel-susten-loop/), are normally open only from June to October, so they will have to wait until next summer. For other shows and owners' meetings this winter, see our [events calendar](/events/).
