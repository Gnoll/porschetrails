Porsche Cars North America announced on 20 October that the Porsche Track Experience (PTX), its driving school in the United States, will add two new courses in 2027. Both are built around the 911 GT3, and Porsche says this is the first time a whole PTX programme has been given over to a single model.

## The two new courses

Both new courses run at Barber Motorsports Park in Birmingham, Alabama.

**GT Drift** is a half-day course on car control when the front and rear tyres are no longer travelling in the same direction. It starts with a classroom session on vehicle dynamics, moves to the skid pad, and then works through trail braking, rotation and drifting. It costs $1,500 per participant.

**Masters GT** is a three-day advanced programme, offered by invitation only to graduates of the existing Masters RS course. Day one is spent on the Barber Proving Grounds working on advanced vehicle control. Days two and three are on the circuit, alternating open lapping with data review and individual coaching. It costs $23,000 per participant, and Porsche describes it as the highest level of instruction PTX offers.

## What stays the same

The core curriculum continues at Barber and at WeatherTech Raceway Laguna Seca in California, which became the school's second North American home in 2026. The existing courses and their 2027 prices are:

- **Precision** (one day): $2,500 at Barber, $3,600 at Laguna Seca
- **Performance** (two days): $5,000 and $6,700
- **Masters** (three days): $6,600 and $9,200
- **Masters RS** (three days): $10,600 and $14,300

Depending on the course, students drive the [718 Cayman and Boxster GTS 4.0](/models/718-981/), the 911 Carrera GTS or the [911 GT3](/models/911-992/). The school has been at Barber since 2003, when it opened as the Porsche Sport Driving School. Both circuits are listed on our [tracks page](/tracks/).

## Why it matters to owners

A school course is one of the most useful things an owner can buy. You learn how a Porsche behaves at and beyond the limit in the company's own cars, with instructors, run-off and no oncoming traffic. Skid-pad work in particular is hard to find anywhere else, and what it teaches about weight transfer and recovering a slide carries over to wet or greasy roads at ordinary speeds.

If you then want to take your own car on track, our guide to [track day modifications](/guides/track-day-modifications/) covers what is worth changing first, starting with brake fluid and pads rather than power.

Porsche has not said in the announcement when 2027 dates open. Bookings and enquiries go through [porschedriving.com](https://www.porschedriving.com/).
